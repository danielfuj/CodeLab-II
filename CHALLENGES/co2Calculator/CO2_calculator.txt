CO2 Calculator
Features
Transport emissions – Estimate CO₂ from travel by car, bus, train, or flight
Electricity emissions – Estimate CO₂ from household/grid electricity usage
Book printing estimate – Rough CO₂ impact based on number of pages (useful alongside the Book Manager project)
All emission factors are approximate and intended for educational/demonstration purposes only.
Emission Factors Used
Activity	Factor	Notes
Car (petrol)	0.20 kg CO₂e / km	Average passenger car
Bus	0.10 kg CO₂e / km	Shared public transport
Train	0.04 kg CO₂e / km	Electric/rail average
Flight	0.25 kg CO₂e / km	Short-to-medium haul average
Electricity	0.40 kg CO₂e / kWh	Varies significantly by country
Book printing	~0.0007 kg / page	Rough paper + printing estimate
